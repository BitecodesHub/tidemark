## Executive summary
- This checklist lists every Phase 0 deliverable and acceptance criterion from the concept document, with an owner, an hour estimate and a target week. The estimates sum to 32.75 hours: 11.20 in week 1, 11.20 in week 2 and 10.35 in week 3; **31.75 hours after the PD-6 deferral of D2 and D7.**
- **The overrun is settled (PD-6):** D2 and D7 move to Phase 1 week 4 and the remaining 1.75-hour overrun is accepted, with actual hours tracked weekly. A realistic range stays 32 to 40 hours if the AI drafts need substantial rework.
- The concept splits Phase 0 into 21 building hours and 9 other hours. This checklist allocates 17.5 building hours and 15.25 other hours, because 8 interviews with their per-interview records need about 10.5 hours.
- Two kill criteria are decided on Sunday 8 November 2026: kernel label raising (criterion 1) and interview evidence (criterion 2). Section 6 holds the decision-record template. **The concept's full wording governs both criteria and a triggered criterion cannot be overridden (PD-7, recorded 2026-10-23, before the x86_64 run).** Criterion 1 is 16 of 16 on **both** architectures as of 2026-10-23 - aarch64 in three Lima runs, x86_64 in four GitHub Actions QEMU/KVM runs, with overlayfs covered on each - and no gated check has failed; criterion 2 stands at zero of eight interviews. Neither criterion has been *evaluated*: that is the founder's record on 8 November (I1), and the section 6 table is now evidence-filled for it.
- One kernel fact changed the plan's wording and then changed back: `bpf_real_data_inode` exists from Linux 7.2, not 7.1, and the labeller used to depend on it. **It no longer does (PD-1: no inode-keyed cache), so the 7.2 floor is now a testing-coverage choice rather than a kfunc requirement (PD-2).** Fedora 44 and 45 ship 7.2 kernels (section 10).
- **The image pipeline is real and green except where it was switched off on purpose.** Later on 2026-10-23: both `:45` and `:44` x86_64 images build from re-pinned base digests in about 6 minutes (E1, T1, P1), each bootc-image-builder qcow2 boots twice under QEMU/KVM with the in-image self-test passing at 9 to 11 seconds of guest uptime on **both** variants - the `:44` fallback green in CI for the first time (T4, P2, K1 to K3) - and each build produces a syft SPDX SBOM plus a report-only trivy scan (S3). Run 39982982294 then ran the graph to completion with `sign and publish` skipped by its own gate, which is the PD-17 design observed working rather than asserted. Three items stay open with named reasons rather than narrowed definitions: the GHCR push and `cosign verify` are opt-in because keyless signing writes an irreversible public transparency-log entry for a private repository (PD-17, so AC6, E2 and S1 remain open); the boot test does not yet run the BPF probe because the probe is not in the image, which is D2, deferred by PD-6 (so E3 and T2 remain open); and branch protection is deliberately off while this is a solo repository, with secret scanning simply unavailable on this plan (PD-18, so E4 and S2 remain open).

## 2. How to read this list
- **Owner: Founder.** The founder does the work and makes the decision.

- **Hours** are founder hours, including review. Where an AI draft already exists, the hours cover review, correction and verification, not authoring.
- **Evidence** names the file or CI artifact that proves the item. Tick a box only when that evidence exists.
